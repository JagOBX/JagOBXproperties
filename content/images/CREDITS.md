Every photo in this folder is either owned by JAGOBX Properties or has been
released into the public domain by its rights holder. Nothing here is used on
a "probably fine" assumption. Before adding a file, record its source and
license below.

A note on National Park Service material: work produced by NPS staff is in the
public domain, but images the NPS publishes that are credited to an outside
photographer or partner are not. The credit line is the test. Check it before
you use one.

Locator map diagrams are generated at build time by `scripts/locator-map.mjs`
and are not stored in this folder.
