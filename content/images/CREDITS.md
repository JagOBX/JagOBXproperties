# Article image credits

Every image in this folder is either owned by JAGOBX Properties, in the public
domain, or licensed under Creative Commons in a way that permits commercial use
with attribution. Nothing here is used on a "probably fine" assumption.

Two rules follow from that:

1. Before adding a file, open its source page and read the licence yourself.
   Record it below. "Found on Google" is not a licence.
2. Any file marked CC must carry a visible credit in its caption on the page,
   naming the photographer and the licence. The article Markdown does this
   through the image title text:

       ![alt text](/images/file.jpg "Caption. Photo by Name, CC BY-SA 4.0.")

A note on National Park Service material: work produced by NPS staff is in the
public domain, but images the NPS publishes that are credited to an outside
photographer or partner are not. The credit line is the test.

A note on CC BY-SA: share alike applies to modified versions of the photograph
itself, not to the article or the website it appears on. Cropping and resizing
for the web is fine. The obligation is the credit.
