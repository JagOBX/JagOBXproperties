Every Outer Banks trip has one morning where the rain sets in and the beach is
off. Everyone has the same idea at the same time, and they all drive to Manteo.

That is worth knowing before you go, because the aquarium now runs on timed
entry, and the slots on a wet morning go early. Book from the house before you
have decided anything else about the day.

This is a mid-sized regional aquarium, not a city-scale one. Two to three hours
is a full visit. Arrive expecting to fill a whole day and you will be
disappointed. Arrive expecting an hour and you will be rushing. It sits on
Airport Road outside Manteo, set back among pines, which is why people drive
past the turn.

## The big tank is the reason to come

The centrepiece is a large ocean tank with sharks in it, lit blue and deep
enough that the fish pass well above your head. Children stand at that glass
far longer than at anything else in the building, and adults do too.

Go there first. It is the busiest spot in the aquarium and it thins out
noticeably in the last hour before closing.

![A shark gliding through deep blue water in a large tank](/images/aquarium-shark.jpg "The tank is deep enough that the sharks pass overhead. Photo by Royian, CC BY-SA 4.0.")

## The otters earn their billing

River otters are the other thing people remember, and the aquarium runs an otter
enrichment session as one of its free daily programmes. That is the one worth
timing your visit around, because otters asleep behind a rock are a very
different experience from otters being given something to solve.

Ask at the ticket window for the day's programme times when you arrive. They are
included with admission and they are the difference between a good visit and a
walk past some glass.

![A river otter on a rock at the edge of its pool](/images/aquarium-otter.jpg "Otter enrichment is a scheduled daily programme, not a chance sighting. Photo by Captain-tucker, CC BY-SA 3.0.")

## The freshwater tanks are quietly the best part

The coastal North Carolina habitats get less attention than the sharks and
deserve more. Yellow-bellied sliders, painted turtles and the rest of the
blackwater and estuary species are the animals actually living in the water your
rental backs onto, which makes them more interesting than they sound.

If your group splits, this is the calm end of the building.

![Two yellow-bellied slider turtles resting on a log above the water](/images/aquarium-sliders.jpg "Sliders like these live in the same sound water you can see from the road in. Photo by Captain-tucker, CC BY-SA 3.0.")

## The sea turtle hospital is the part worth explaining to children

The Sea Turtle Assistance and Rehabilitation Center, the STAR Center, is a
working rehabilitation facility rather than an exhibit. Turtles come in cold
stunned, injured by boats or tangled in gear, and the ones you see are patients
that will be released.

It keeps shorter hours than the rest of the building, 9.30 a.m. to 4.30 p.m., so
do not leave it until last. This is the part of the visit that sticks with kids,
and it makes the point about fishing line and plastic better than any sign does.

![A bronze sculpture of a sea turtle on the aquarium grounds](/images/aquarium-turtle-statue.jpg "The STAR Center treats live strandings and releases what it can. Photo by Chelsea Miller, CC BY-SA 4.0.")

## Practical notes

The address is 374 Airport Road, Manteo, and the number is (252) 475-2300. Open
daily 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

One oddity worth flagging: **the aquarium does not publish its admission prices
on its website.** They only appear once you are inside the booking system
choosing a date and time. Taxes are added separately, and any discounted ticket
needs matching identification at the door, so bring it.

Arrive at your reserved time rather than early. On busy days they cannot take
early arrivals, and you check in at the ticket window before going through.

Prices and programme times change, so book and check on the
[official aquarium page](https://www.ncaquariums.com/roanoke-island)
before you drive over.
