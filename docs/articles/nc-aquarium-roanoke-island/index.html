NC Aquarium on Roanoke Island: The Rainy Day Plan

The place everyone on the Outer Banks ends up when the forecast turns, and the one attraction here that works better in bad weather than good.

Published September 8, 2026 · 6 min read

In short

The North Carolina Aquarium on Roanoke Island is at 374 Airport Road in Manteo, open daily 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. and closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. Entry is timed and reserved tickets are required, with the last reservation at 4 p.m. and last entry at 4.30 p.m. Time slots sell out in summer and on wet days. Admission prices are not listed on the aquarium website, only inside the booking system. Allow two to three hours.

Every Outer Banks trip has one morning where the rain sets in and the beach is off. Everyone has the same idea at the same time, and they all drive to Manteo.

That is worth knowing before you go, because the aquarium now runs on timed entry, and the slots on a wet morning go early. Book from the house before you have decided anything else about the day.

This is a mid-sized regional aquarium, not a city-scale one. Two to three hours is a full visit. Arrive expecting to fill a whole day and you will be disappointed. Arrive expecting an hour and you will be rushing. It sits on Airport Road outside Manteo, set back among pines, which is why people drive past the turn.

The big tank is the reason to come

The centrepiece is a large ocean tank with sharks in it, lit blue and deep enough that the fish pass well above your head. Children stand at that glass far longer than at anything else in the building, and adults do too.

Go there first. It is the busiest spot in the aquarium and it thins out noticeably in the last hour before closing.

The otters earn their billing

River otters are the other thing people remember, and the aquarium runs an otter enrichment session as one of its free daily programmes. That is the one worth timing your visit around, because otters asleep behind a rock are a very different experience from otters being given something to solve.

Ask at the ticket window for the day's programme times when you arrive. They are included with admission and they are the difference between a good visit and a walk past some glass.

The freshwater tanks are quietly the best part

The coastal North Carolina habitats get less attention than the sharks and deserve more. Yellow-bellied sliders, painted turtles and the rest of the blackwater and estuary species are the animals actually living in the water your rental backs onto, which makes them more interesting than they sound.

If your group splits, this is the calm end of the building.

The sea turtle hospital is the part worth explaining to children

The Sea Turtle Assistance and Rehabilitation Center, the STAR Center, is a working rehabilitation facility rather than an exhibit. Turtles come in cold stunned, injured by boats or tangled in gear, and the ones you see are patients that will be released.

It keeps shorter hours than the rest of the building, 9.30 a.m. to 4.30 p.m., so do not leave it until last. This is the part of the visit that sticks with kids, and it makes the point about fishing line and plastic better than any sign does.

Practical notes

The address is 374 Airport Road, Manteo, and the number is (252) 475-2300. Open daily 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

One oddity worth flagging: the aquarium does not publish its admission prices on its website. They only appear once you are inside the booking system choosing a date and time. Taxes are added separately, and any discounted ticket needs matching identification at the door, so bring it.

Arrive at your reserved time rather than early. On busy days they cannot take early arrivals, and you check in at the ticket window before going through.

Prices and programme times change, so book and check on the official aquarium page before you drive over.

Common questions

Do you need to book tickets in advance?

Yes. Entry is timed and reserved tickets are required rather than merely recommended. The last reservation slot is 4 p.m. and last entry is 4.30 p.m. Slots sell out, and the first properly wet morning of the week is when that happens.

How much does it cost?

The aquarium does not publish its admission prices on its website. They appear inside the booking system when you choose a date and time. Taxes are added on top, and discounted tickets require matching identification at the door.

What are the hours?

Daily, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. The Sea Turtle Assistance and Rehabilitation Center keeps shorter hours, 9.30 a.m. to 4.30 p.m. The aquarium closes on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

How long should we allow?

Two to three hours covers it at a normal pace, longer if you stay for the daily programmes. It is a mid-sized aquarium rather than a whole-day attraction, which suits a morning that got rained out.

Is anything included beyond the tanks?

Yes. Several free daily programmes come with regular admission, including a Creature Feature, otter enrichment and shipwreck talks. Ask for the day's schedule at the ticket window and plan around the times rather than discovering them on the way out.

Staying nearby?

Manteo is about 25 minutes from our places in Kill Devil Hills, straight down the bypass and over the bridge.
